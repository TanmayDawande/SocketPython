RSA encryption
Two distinct prime numbers P and Q are generated and multiplied to generate N. this number is the public key. A function called the Euler's Totient function is defined as
ϕ(N) = (P-1)*(Q-1). This functions basically spits out all the numbers co-prime with N(numbers that do not share a common factor).
To pick one of the private key, lets say, e, it must meet these two criterias:-
-- 1 < e < ϕ(N)
-- coprime with N, ϕ(N)

to chose the private key in the decrypt side, lets say, d, we gotta ensure that:

--d*e(mod(ϕ(N))) == 1

This is RSA encryption.
